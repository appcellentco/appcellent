Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Limify, from setting up purchase limits to managing inventory thresholds on your Shopify store.

General

What is Limify?

Limify is a Shopify app that lets merchants set product purchase limits, order quantity restrictions, and stock-based rules to control customer purchasing behavior and protect inventory.

How do I install Limify?

Install Limify directly from the Shopify App Store. Once installed, it appears in your Shopify admin panel under Apps. No coding or theme modifications are required.

Is Limify free to use?

Pricing details are available on the Shopify App Store listing. Visit the app listing for current plans and pricing information.

What Shopify plans does Limify support?

Limify works with all Shopify plans including Basic, Shopify, Advanced, and Shopify Plus.

Features & Limits

What types of limits can I set?

Limify supports three main types of limits:

  • Product purchase limits (maximum quantity per product or variant)
  • Order quantity limits (minimum and maximum per order)
  • Stock threshold rules (automatic limits based on inventory levels)
Can I set limits per product or per variant?

Yes. Limits can be configured at both the product level and the individual variant level, giving you granular control over each item in your store.

Do limits apply per customer or per order?

You can configure limits to apply per order, per customer, or both. This flexibility lets you prevent bulk buying while still allowing reasonable purchases.

Can I set different limits for different products?

Yes. Each product or variant can have its own unique limit configuration. You can also apply bulk rules to collections or your entire catalog.

How do stock threshold rules work?

Stock threshold rules automatically adjust purchase limits based on your current inventory levels. For example, you can set tighter limits when stock drops below a certain quantity, helping prevent overselling during high-demand periods.

Privacy & Data

What data does Limify access?

Limify accesses your Shopify store data (products, orders, inventory) through the Shopify API to enforce limits. No data is shared with third parties beyond Shopify. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@appcellent.co. Upon uninstalling the app, your store data is automatically deleted within 30 days.

Troubleshooting

Limits are not being enforced.

  • Verify that the app is enabled in your Shopify admin.
  • Check that limits are configured for the specific product or variant.
  • Ensure the app block or snippet is active in your theme.
  • Clear your store's cache and test in an incognito browser window.
  • If the issue persists, contact us at support@appcellent.co.

App is not appearing in Shopify admin.

  • Try reinstalling the app from the Shopify App Store.
  • Make sure you are logged in as the store owner or a staff member with app permissions.
  • Check that your Shopify plan supports third-party apps.
  • If the issue persists, contact us at support@appcellent.co.

Customers can still exceed the set limits.

  • Verify the limit type is set correctly (per order vs. per customer).
  • Check if the customer is placing multiple separate orders to bypass per-order limits.
  • Review your limit rules for any conflicting configurations.
  • Ensure the app's theme integration is properly installed.
