Show Shoppers Exactly When Their Order Arrives

Getby displays accurate "Get it by" delivery dates on your product and cart pages — with a live countdown to your order cutoff. Fewer support tickets, more confident buyers.

Coming Soon on Shopify

Features

📅 Accurate Dates

Order cutoff time, working days, holidays and your shop's timezone are all taken into account — so the promise on the page matches reality.

⏳ Cutoff Countdown

A live "order within 2h 14m" countdown nudges shoppers to buy today instead of tomorrow.

🎯 Flexible Rules

Target delivery times by product, collection, tag or destination country, with exclusions and a default rule as fallback.

🎨 Matches Your Theme

Ready-made design presets, custom colors, fonts and per-language translations. Installs as a theme app block — no code edits.

🛒 Product & Cart Pages

Show estimates where the decision happens: on the product page and again in the cart.

🧹 Clean Uninstall

Everything lives in a theme app extension. Uninstall the app and nothing is left behind in your theme.

How It Works

1. Create rules

Start with a default delivery time, then add targeted rules for specific products, collections, tags or countries.

2. Add the widget

One click opens your theme editor with the "Delivery estimate" block pre-added. Hit Save — that's it.

3. Publish

Preview safely in draft mode, then publish when you're ready. Changes go live instantly.
