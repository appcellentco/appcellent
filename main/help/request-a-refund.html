How to Request a Refund

All purchases made through the App Store — including subscriptions and one-time purchases — are handled by Apple. Because of this, refund requests must be submitted directly to Apple rather than to us.

Request a refund on the web

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Find the purchase you want refunded.
  4. Choose "I'd like to", then select Request a refund.
  5. Choose a reason and submit your request.

Request a refund on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app and tap your name (Apple ID).
  2. Tap Media & Purchases, then Purchase History.
  3. Find the purchase, then tap Report a Problem.
  4. Follow the prompts to request your refund.

What happens next

Apple will review your request and notify you by email, usually within a few business days. If approved, the refund is returned to your original payment method.

Good to know

  • We can't process refunds directly or guarantee Apple's decision — approvals are made by Apple.
  • Apple's decision can depend on how recently you bought the item, how much you've used the app, and the reason you provide.
  • Submit your request soon after purchase and give a clear reason to help Apple review it.
  • If your request is denied, you can contact Apple Support for more help.
