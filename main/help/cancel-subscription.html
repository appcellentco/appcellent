How to Cancel Your Subscription

All subscriptions purchased through the App Store are managed entirely by Apple. We do not have the ability to cancel subscriptions on your behalf — you need to do this yourself through your Apple ID settings.

How to cancel on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app on your device.
  2. Tap your name (Apple ID) at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Find and tap the app you want to cancel in the list.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.
  6. Confirm the cancellation.

Important notes

  • Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period, even after you cancel.
  • If you don't see a Cancel Subscription option, your subscription may already be canceled.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription — you must follow the steps above.

Why can't you cancel it for me?

When you subscribe through the App Store, your payment and subscription are processed directly by Apple. This means:

  • We never receive your payment information.
  • We don't have access to your Apple ID or subscription settings.
  • Only you (or Apple) can manage subscriptions tied to your Apple ID.

Need to manage billing directly with Apple? Visit Apple Support.
