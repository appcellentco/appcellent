Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Colorzy, from creating coloring pages to subscriptions and printing.

General

What is Colorzy?

Colorzy is an iOS app that turns your photos and ideas into clean, printable coloring pages using AI. You can convert a photo into a coloring page, generate one from a text description, browse a gallery of ready-made templates, and print or export your results.

Is Colorzy free to use?

The app is free to download and includes one free coloring page generation so you can try it out. To create unlimited coloring pages and unlock the full template gallery and high-quality print & export, a premium subscription is required.

What devices are supported?

Colorzy is available for iPhone running iOS 16 or later.

Creating Coloring Pages

How do I turn a photo into a coloring page?

Choose the Photo to Coloring Page option, select a photo from your library, and our AI will convert it into a clean line-art coloring page in seconds.

Can I create a coloring page without a photo?

Yes. With Text to Coloring Page, you simply describe what you want — for example, "a friendly dinosaur in a jungle" — and the AI draws an original coloring page from your description.

Are the coloring pages original?

Every page is generated specifically from your photo or prompt. Because AI results can vary, similar pages may occasionally be created for different users.

What is the template gallery?

The template gallery is a growing library of curated, ready-made coloring pages across themes like animals, flowers, cartoons, and holidays — so you can start coloring right away.

Printing & Export

Can I print my coloring pages?

Yes. You can print your coloring pages directly from the app via AirPrint, or export them as high-quality images or PDFs to print later.

Subscription

What does the premium subscription include?

Premium unlocks unlimited Photo to Coloring Page and Text to Coloring Page generation, the full template gallery, and high-quality print & export.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID Account Settings. The subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Privacy

What happens to the photos I upload?

The photos and prompts you submit are sent securely to our cloud and AI providers only to generate your coloring page, and are deleted from our processing systems afterward. We do not sell your photos. See our Privacy Policy for details.
