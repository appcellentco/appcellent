Keep Score Like a Pro

A full darts scoreboard in your pocket. Play X01, Cricket, Around the Clock and Party Killer with automatic scoring, checkout suggestions, player statistics and a record of every match.

Download on the App Store

Features

🎯 X01: 301, 501 & 701

The classic games with Straight, Double or Master out, optional Double In, and Best of or First to leg formats. Busts are detected automatically.

🧮 Fast Scoring Numpad

Enter a whole visit in seconds. Auto-confirm keeps the game moving, undo fixes slips, and the subtraction is always right.

🏁 Checkout Suggestions

On a finish? The recommended route appears right on the scoreboard, so you always know what to aim at next.

🏏 Cricket

The full mark-and-close scoreboard for 15–20 and Bull, in Standard, Cut-Throat and No Points variants, with MPR tracked for you.

🕐 Around the Clock

Race around the board from 1 to 20 — Bull optional — with house rules for skipping on doubles and triples and an exact-finish mode.

💀 Party Killer

The party classic for three to six players. Claim your number, become the Killer, hit doubles and take lives until one player is left standing.

📊 Player Statistics

3-dart average, checkout rate, 180s, best leg, MPR, win rate and streaks — all computed from your real matches.

🕘 Match History & Resume

Every match is saved automatically. Review finished games or pick up a live match exactly where you left off.

📖 Rules Built In

Every mode ships with a plain-English how-to-play guide, so even first-time players can join in straight away.

Built for Every Kind of Game Night

League practice at home, a soft-tip session at the pub, or a full evening of Killer with five friends passing one phone around the oche — Darts Scorer keeps the maths right and the game moving. It works entirely on your device: no account, no sign-up, no internet connection needed.
